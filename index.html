The Museum Mystery

A digital escape room for 3º ESO · Art, English and critical thinking

Briefing: the museum doors have locked.

The Moonstone Mask has disappeared from the main gallery. Your team is trapped inside the museum after closing time. To escape, you must collect clues, solve language challenges and identify the thief.

This activity has five rooms: vocabulary, grammar, reading, listening and a Montessori-inspired final password.

Mission: take notes. Some words from each room will be needed later to open the final door.

Set up your team

The pre-test gives a suggested level. You can still choose A, B or C in the briefing.

🔐 Final objective: escape the museum and answer: Who stole the Moonstone Mask?

Fase 1 · Briefing and level selection

Your group level adapts the support you receive during the escape.

Group A

More hints, clearer instructions and more visible support.

Group B

Balanced difficulty with some guidance and standard tasks.

Group C

More autonomy, fewer hints and more inferential decisions.

Room 2 · Grammar: The Timeline Corridor

Choose the correct verb form to reconstruct the night of the robbery. Think about completed actions and background actions.

Room 3 · Reading: Cluedo in the Museum

Read the suspect cards and the evidence board. Then choose the most logical conclusions.

1. Ms Green · Curator

“I locked Gallery 2 at eight o’clock and went straight to my office. I was writing an email when the alarm rang.”

Evidence: her computer was active at 8:11.

2. Leo Park · Security guard

“I was in the control room. One camera stopped working for one minute, so I was checking the system.”

Evidence: the control-room log confirms he was there.

3. Clara Moon · Painter

“I was preparing my exhibition in the studio. I carried a large portfolio because my paintings are fragile.”

Evidence: blue paint was found near the studio, not near the mask case.

4. Mr Blake · Art critic

“I left the museum before eight. I did not see anything unusual.”

Evidence: his visitor pass was used at the side door at 8:28.

Evidence board

The Moonstone Mask disappeared at about 8:30. The main door was locked. Dusty black boot prints were found near the side stairs. A theatre ticket was found under the side-door mat.

Room 4 · Listening: The Police Message

Listen to the police conversation. In this HTML version, the listening is generated by your browser using text-to-speech. If your browser blocks it, use the transcript button as a fallback.

Teacher notes

This prototype follows the structure in the design document: pre-test, briefing, adaptive group selection, five museum rooms, final accusation, post-test and debriefing.

Listening honesty: this HTML does not include a recorded audio file. It uses browser text-to-speech, so it works in many modern browsers but may depend on device/browser permissions. A transcript fallback is included.

Suggested classroom use: groups of 2–3 students, one shared device, notebooks for clues, teacher-led debriefing after completion.

Answers: final password: THE THIEF HID THE MASK UNDER THE STAIRS. Final thief: Mr Blake / the art critic.
